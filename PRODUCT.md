# Product

## Register

product

## Users

cueq serves all repository-defined roles at a German university: employees,
team leads, shift planners, HR, payroll, administrators, data-protection staff,
and the works council. They work with sensitive employment records in focused,
often time-critical operational sessions. Their primary job is to understand
current state, resolve the next permitted task, and leave an auditable result
without exposing more personal information than the task requires.

## Product Purpose

cueq brings time tracking, absence management, rosters, approvals, monthly
closing, payroll export, reporting, policy administration, and audit evidence
into one permission-aware workspace. Success means that each role sees the
right work and explanation at the right time, state transitions remain
truthful, and sensitive operations are precise, reviewable, and reversible
where policy permits.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. The product should feel like an institutional
operations desk: disciplined enough for payroll and audit work, clear enough
for occasional employee use, and humane without becoming casual.

## Anti-references

- SaaS gamification, competitive productivity scores, surveillance metrics,
  and attention-seeking dashboards.
- Marketing hero layouts, oversized metric cards, action clutter, and
  decorative empty space that pushes operational evidence below the fold.
- Glassmorphism, blur, gradients, gradient text, broad ambient shadows,
  oversized radii, bounce motion, and decorative animation.
- Interfaces that imply permission through visible controls when the current
  role is not authorized, or hide why an authorized action is temporarily
  unavailable.

## Design Principles

1. **Permission truth.** Navigation, data, and actions reflect the persisted
   role and organizational scope; absence is clearer than a misleading control.
2. **State before action.** Current status, prerequisites, and consequences are
   visible before a person acts.
3. **Privacy by minimization.** Reveal only the personal detail required for
   the active workflow, especially absence reasons and audit records.
4. **Institutional clarity.** Dense operational information remains readable,
   consistent, and German-first without adopting consumer-SaaS theatrics.
5. **Auditable confidence.** Success, failure, stale data, and connectivity are
   explicit, durable, and never disguised by optimistic presentation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA in light and dark themes. All workflows must support keyboard
navigation, visible focus, zoom and narrow screens, reduced motion, non-color
state cues, German-first copy with complete English parity, and privacy-safe
loading, empty, stale, offline, error, disabled, and success states.
